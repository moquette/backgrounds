# Backgrounds

The background photo feed for our TVs, served at https://moquette.github.io/backgrounds/: `manifest.json` lists the photos every TV holds today
and `collection.json` every photo in the collection. The photos come from Wikimedia Commons, each under its own
licence (CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA), not under any licence of this repository. Changed: cropped to
16:9 and resized. Written by `fetch.py --publish`; a photo deleted from `images/` leaves for good.
